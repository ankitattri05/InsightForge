# Data Dictionary

## Overview

This document describes the business metrics, deterministic interpretations, and diagnostics used by InsightForge.

All metrics are calculated by the deterministic analytics engine. Business interpretations are generated by deterministic Python rules, while the Executive Narration Layer communicates only verified findings.

No analytical calculations are performed by the language model.

## Core Business Metrics

| Metric | Description | Business Meaning | Status |
|--------|-------------|------------------|--------|
| Sales | Total sales revenue | Total revenue generated during the reporting period | Descriptive |
| Profit | Total profit | Total profit generated during the reporting period | Descriptive |
| Profit Margin | Profit as a percentage of sales | Percentage of sales retained as profit | Descriptive |
| Average Order Value | Average revenue per order | Average revenue generated from each customer order | Descriptive |
| Orders | Total customer orders | Total customer orders handled during the reporting period | Descriptive |
| Quantity | Total units sold | Total product units sold during the reporting period | Descriptive |
| Average Shipping Days | Average delivery time | Average shipping time required to deliver customer orders | Descriptive |
| Shipping Cost | Total shipping expenditure | Total shipping cost incurred during the reporting period | Descriptive |

## Deterministic Diagnostics

| Diagnostic | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| Top Sales Market | Markets ranked by total sales revenue |
| Top Profit Category | Categories ranked by total profit |
| Bottom Profit Subcategory | Subcategories ranked by profit in ascending order |
| Top Shipping Cost Ship Mode | Ship modes ranked by total shipping cost |

## Business Interpretation Rules

- Every KPI is calculated by the deterministic analytics engine.
- Business interpretations are generated by predefined Python rules.
- Diagnostic rankings are produced from verified SQL aggregations.
- The Executive Narration Layer does not calculate, modify, or reinterpret analytical findings.
- All reported values are traceable to deterministic analytics.

## Diagnostic Definitions

### Top Sales Market
Identifies the markets generating the highest total sales revenue during the reporting period.

### Top Profit Category
Identifies the product categories generating the highest total profit.

### Bottom Profit Subcategory
Identifies the product subcategories with the lowest total profit, including negative profit where applicable.

### Top Shipping Cost Ship Mode
Identifies the ship modes contributing the highest total shipping cost.

## Reporting Principles

InsightForge follows an evidence-first reporting model.

- Every reported metric originates from deterministic SQL and Python analytics.
- Business interpretations are predefined and reproducible.
- The Executive Narration Layer communicates verified findings without generating new analytical conclusions.
- Historical trends, classifications, and causal relationships are reported only when explicitly supported by deterministic analytics.
- If supporting evidence is unavailable, findings are reported descriptively without inference.
