# InsightForge

## AI Business Analysis Studio

InsightForge is a configuration-driven AI Business Analysis platform designed to generate trustworthy executive business narratives from verified analytical results.

The platform follows a deterministic analytics-first architecture:

- MySQL performs aggregation.
- Python computes business metrics.
- AI interprets verified findings.
- AI never invents business facts.

The flagship implementation uses a Telecom Service Assurance dataset, while the analytics engine itself remains domain-independent through semantic configuration.
